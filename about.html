Alistair Norfleet

Political Science & Philosophy

Alistair Norfleet is a Political Science and Philosophy student at the University of Pittsburgh's Frederick Honors College, with a minor in Law, Criminal Justice, and Society. His academic work spans political economy, feminist theory, democratic reform, and the history of philosophy.

Beyond the classroom, Alistair coaches parliamentary debate, judges Model EU tournaments, and has managed legal research for the KIEZconnect initiative in Berlin. His drawings — rendered in graphite and ink — offer a creative counterpoint to his analytical writing.

He will be joining the University of Pittsburgh School of Law in Fall 2026.
