Alistair Norfleet

Philosopher & Artist

Alistair Norfleet is a philosopher and artist whose work sits at the intersection of phenomenology, ethics, and the philosophy of mind. His essays explore what it means to be a conscious, embodied creature navigating a world that often exceeds our capacity to describe it.

Trained in the analytic and continental traditions, Alistair writes with a commitment to clarity without reductionism — to making difficult ideas available without making them thin. His art practice, which spans oil painting, graphite, and mixed media, explores the same themes through image and materiality.

He is currently based in New York.
